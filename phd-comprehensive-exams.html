At a glance

WhoAll PhD students.
WhenAt or near, but no later than 12 months after, completion of the formal courses in the approved Program of Study.
Before schedulingA complete doctoral committee must be established with Graduate School approval.
Scheduling formSubmit at least two weeks before the written examination begins.
Written examinationCompleted first; at least eight hours. The committee may allow up to one week.
Oral examinationOnly after passing the written examination; normally within four weeks; no more than two hours, excluding deliberations.
PassingBoth parts require unanimous approval by the applicable committee.
ValidityPassing written and oral results remain valid for five years.

What students need to do

  1. Establish the doctoral committee

    Before scheduling the examination, make sure the complete doctoral committee has been approved by the Graduate School. The oral examination committee also includes an additional impartial Graduate Faculty member from a different department.

  2. Coordinate the written and oral dates

    Work with the major professor and committee members to identify the written start date and a planned oral date. The oral date is contingent upon passing the written examination and receiving permission from the Dean of the Graduate School.

  3. Submit the scheduling form

    Submit the current Graduate School form at least two weeks before the written examination begins. Include both the written start date and the planned oral date.

  4. Complete the written examination

    The written examination lasts at least eight hours and may extend up to one week. Its format is set by the major professor in consultation with the doctoral committee. Unanimous committee approval is required to pass.

  5. Complete the oral examination

    After passing the written examination, complete the oral examination, normally within four weeks. It may last no more than two hours, excluding committee deliberations. Unanimous approval of the oral examination committee is required to pass.

  6. Confirm that both results were reported

    The written and oral results are reported separately to the Graduate School. A student passes the Comprehensive Examination only after passing both parts.

If an examination is failed: reexamination is not automatic. It requires the applicable committee's recommendation and the Dean of the Graduate School's approval.
