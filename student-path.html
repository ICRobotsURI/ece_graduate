Graduation milestones

The sequence of planning, forms, examinations, proposals, defenses, and deadlines from the beginning of the program through graduation.

Your first semester

  • Confirm your faculty advisor or major professor.
  • Register for the appropriate courses and required seminar.
  • Read the requirements for your exact degree option.
  • Begin your Program of Study; do not postpone planning until graduation.
  • Discuss funding expectations directly with the relevant faculty or department contact.
  • If your path requires research compliance training, complete it early.

MS milestone map

Read the complete MS degree requirements, including credit and option-specific rules.

All MS students

  1. Choose the correct option

    Thesis and non-thesis paths have different culminating work and forms.

  2. Submit the Program of Study

    List the credits that will satisfy all departmental and Graduate School requirements.

  3. Complete coursework and seminar obligations

    Monitor both total credits and category-specific requirements.

  4. Finish the culminating work

    Complete ELE 598 for non-thesis, or proposal, research, thesis, and defense for thesis.

  5. Apply for graduation

    Submit nomination and completion materials by the calendar deadlines.

PhD milestone map

Read the complete PhD degree requirements. See the qualifying examination page for the current format, schedule, registration procedure, and deadlines.

  1. Identify a major professor

    Your research direction, funding, and doctoral plan depend heavily on this relationship.

  2. Establish the committee and Program of Study

    Formalize who guides the degree and which credits satisfy it.

  3. Pass the qualifying examination

    Follow the current departmental schedule and registration instructions on the qualifying examination page.

  4. Complete the written and oral comprehensive examinations

    Review the committee, scheduling, format, and result procedures on the comprehensive examinations page.

  5. Obtain dissertation proposal approval

    Complete required research training and approvals before applicable deadlines.

  6. Research, write, and defend

    Schedule backward from Graduate School defense and final-submission dates.

  7. Complete graduation requirements

    Submit nomination, final dissertation, and other required records on time.

Deadlines move every academic year

The Graduate School calendar lists deadlines for Programs of Study, proposals, graduation nominations, defenses, final manuscripts, leaves, reinstatement, and special status requests.

Best practice: at the beginning of each semester, identify every deadline that applies to you and place it on your calendar. Departmental review may require submission earlier than the final Graduate School date.

Stay in good academic standing

Graduate study has minimum grade and GPA expectations, enrollment rules, and maximum completion periods. These can affect degree credit, assistantship status, immigration status, and continued enrollment.

Consult the Graduate School Manual for the official rules, and ask before changing enrollment or taking a leave—especially if you are funded or studying on a visa.
