> **Electrical Engineering M.S. Degree Program Requirements**
>
> **All Program Requirements:**

1.  Minimum of 30 credits in engineering, mathematics, and/or science
    courses.

2.  Minimum of 16 credits in formal graduate electrical engineering
    (ELE) courses, excluding ELE 601 and ELE 602.

3.  Minimum of one credit of departmental seminar (ELE 601 and/or 602).
    Up to two credits of seminar may be used toward the 30-credit
    requirement.

4.  Maximum of 12 credits of senior undergraduate (400-level) courses in
    engineering, mathematics, and/or science.

5.  Maximum of three credits of special problems (“independent study”)
    courses (ELE 591/592/691/692).

6.  All full-time graduate students (M. S. or Ph. D.) are required to
    enroll in ELE 601 every semester the course is offered.

# Thesis Program:

1.  Maximum of 6–9 credits of thesis research (ELE 599). Six credits are
    standard; more than six credits requires thesis committee and
    Graduate Director/Department Chair approval.

# Non-Thesis Program:

1.  Complete ELE 598 Master’s Project.

# Notes:

1.  The Graduate School requires 18 formal course credits.

2.  Programs cannot have more than 12 credits of thesis research
    (ELE 599) and special problems (ELE 591/592/691/692) courses.
    \[section 7.44.1\]

3.  The ELE Graduate Affairs Committee must approve all 400-level
    courses to be counted toward the credit requirements.

4.  Advanced part-time students may substitute ELE 602 (one seminar
    presentation) for ELE 601.

5.  Graduate courses are numbered 500 and above; undergraduate courses
    are numbered 499 and below.

6.  Courses below the 400-level do *not* fulfill any program
    requirements. \[section 9.12\]

7.  To fulfill credit requirements:

    1.  a course grade of B- or higher is required in any 400-level
        course;

    2.  a course grade of C or higher is required in any 500- and
        600-level courses.

8.  Students must be enrolled in at least 1 credit during the graduation
    semester. \[section 4.40\]

> **Electrical Engineering M. S. Degree Program Milestones Graduate
> Manual chapters 7–9, 11** https://web.uri.edu/graduate-manual/

1.  Program of Study \[section 7.43\]

    1.  specifies major professor (thesis: graduate faculty; non-thesis:
        Graduate Director)

    2.  full-time student: submit no later than end of 3rd semester

    3.  part-time student: submit no later than end of 4th semester

    4.  Grad School form: different for thesis or non-thesis

    5.  can be revised, but must match courses on transcript

2.  Thesis Program

    1.  Credit Requirements \[section 7.44.1\] (see page 1)

    2.  Thesis Committee \[section 7.44.15\]

        1.  “Master’s Program Committee” = thesis committee

        2.  thesis committee = major professor, inside dept faculty,
            outside dept faculty

        3.  Grad School form “Establishment of a Masters Committee”

        4.  submitted with or before Program of Study

    3.  Thesis Proposal \[section 7.44.3\]

        1.  University’s required format

        2.  must be submitted during 1st or 2nd semester of research
            credits

        3.  must be submitted at least one semester before thesis is
            submitted/defended

        4.  human subjects: IRB approval; live animals: IACUC approval

        5.  requires completed Responsible Conduct of Research (RCR)
            training \[section 7.44.35\]

        6.  Grad School form “Thesis Proposal Approval Form”

    4.  Grad School “Thesis Format Guidelines”

    5.  Grad School form “Request to Schedule an Oral Defense of a
        Masters Thesis”

    6.  Oral Defense of Thesis \[section 7.44.5\]

        1.  “Thesis Defense Committee” = thesis committee & outside
            member (chair)

        2.  20 days before date of thesis defense: \[section 11.23\]

            -   at least one unbound copy of thesis to Grad School (or,
                enough copies for com- mittee members who want paper
                copy), plus a large envelope

            -   receipt from Enrollment Services for the
                binding/microfilming fee

        3.  not less than 15 days before defense date: student gets
            copies from Grad School, distributes to committee \[section
            11.25\]

        4.  student and major professor must be physically present

        5.  only one committee member may attend electronically

        6.  no more than two hours long

        7.  unanimous approval of the committee is required for passing
            \[section 8.42.3\]

        8.  any interested observers may attend

    7.  if needed: Grad School form “Certification that Mandatory
        Corrections were Made to a Successfully Defended Thesis”
        \[section 11.28\]

3.  Non-Thesis Program \[section 7.45\]

    1.  Credit Requirements \[section 7.45.1\] (see page 1)

    2.  Culminating experience \[section 7.45.2\]:

        1.  ELE 598 Master’s Project (one semester 3-credit project)

4.  Nomination for Graduation

    1.  Grad School form: different for thesis or non-thesis

    2.  usually due 3rd week of the graduation semester

    3.  Students must be enrolled in at least 1 credit during the
        graduation semester. \[section 4.40\]
