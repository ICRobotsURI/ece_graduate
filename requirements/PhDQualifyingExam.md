# Proposed ECE PhD Qualifying Examination Policy

> **Status:** Draft for departmental review. This document is not yet official policy.
>
> **Policy hierarchy:** The University of Rhode Island Graduate School Manual controls. College of Engineering requirements apply next. The Department of Electrical, Computer and Biomedical Engineering may impose additional requirements that do not conflict with either higher-level source.

## 1. Purpose

The PhD Qualifying Examination evaluates whether a student has the potential to perform satisfactorily at the doctoral level.

## 2. Who takes the examination

1. All students admitted to the ECE PhD program are required to take and pass the ECE Qualifying Examination, including students who hold a master's degree.

2. For a student who holds a master's degree in the same or a closely related field, the Department will make the decision to require the examination when the admission application is processed, transmit that recommendation to the Dean of the Graduate School with the admission recommendation, and stipulate the examination requirement at the time of admission.

3. Every ECE PhD student must pass the Qualifying Examination no later than the end of the student's second year in the PhD program.

4. Students normally take their first examination during their third semester. A student who fails that examination may take an authorized reexamination during the following semester, subject to the requirements in Section 7.

## 3. When the examination is offered

1. The Department normally offers the Qualifying Examination once during each fall and spring semester, usually in November and March.

2. The examination is normally held on a Saturday or Sunday morning.

3. The Graduate Program Director will announce the examination date, registration procedure, and registration deadline by email during the first weeks of the semester.

## 4. Registration

1. A student who intends to take the examination must submit the registration form distributed by the Graduate Program Director.

2. A student who does not submit the form by the announced deadline may not take the examination that semester.

3. Registration does not override the timing and eligibility requirements in Section 2.

## 5. Examination format and administration

1. The ECE Qualifying Examination is a written examination scheduled for four hours.

2. The examination is closed book and closed notes.

3. When registering for the examination, each student must identify three eligible ELE graduate courses that the student completed at URI during the student's first two semesters in the PhD program.

4. An eligible course must be an ELE course numbered 500 or above. ELE 594 is eligible. The following courses are not eligible:

   - ELE 591 and ELE 592;
   - ELE 601 and ELE 602; and
   - thesis or dissertation credits ELE 598, ELE 599, and ELE 699.

5. The student will receive two questions corresponding to each of the three selected courses, for a total of six questions.

6. The student must answer four of the six questions. If the student answers more than four questions, the student must clearly identify the four answers to be evaluated. Any other answers will be disregarded.

7. The selected courses define the subject areas of the examination. Completing or passing those courses does not constitute completion of the Qualifying Examination. The Qualifying Examination requirement is satisfied only by passing the separate written examination described in this section.

8. The Department, acting through the Graduate Program Director and department Chair as applicable, is responsible for the preparation and administration of the examination in consultation with either a departmental examination committee or the student's major professor.

9. The Graduate Program Director will provide students with the examination instructions before the examination.

## 6. Results

1. The examiners determine whether the student has passed or failed the examination.

2. The student and the Dean of the Graduate School will be informed promptly of the result using the Graduate School's current reporting procedure and form. The Department will follow the acknowledgment and signature workflow required by the current form.

## 7. Failure and reexamination

1. A student who fails the examination may be permitted **one** reexamination in the failed part or parts.

2. Reexamination is not automatic. It must be recommended by the examiners and approved by the Dean of the Graduate School.

3. An approved reexamination may occur only after at least ten weeks have passed following the first examination and before one year has elapsed.

4. A student who fails the examination during the third semester will normally take an authorized reexamination during the following semester.

5. The Department will recommend dismissal when:

   - a student is not permitted to take a reexamination after failing the first examination;
   - a student fails the authorized reexamination; or
   - a student has not passed the examination by the end of the second year.

6. Any dismissal action remains subject to applicable Graduate School procedures, including the Graduate School's notice and appeal requirements.

## 8. Authority

This proposed policy is based on the hierarchy below:

1. URI Graduate School Manual sections 7.55.1, 7.55.2, and 8.43.1.
2. College of Engineering graduate examination requirements.
3. Additional ECE scheduling, registration, format, and progress requirements stated in this document.
