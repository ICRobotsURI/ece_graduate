Academic planning

Program of Study

The Program of Study lists the courses and credits a student intends to use to satisfy degree requirements. A revised Program of Study is required when the courses used for the degree change.

View instructions

Basic procedure

  • Choose the correct degree requirements.
  • List the courses and research credits that satisfy them.
  • Review the plan with your advisor or major professor.
  • Submit the official Graduate School form on time.
  • Revise it later if your actual coursework changes.
