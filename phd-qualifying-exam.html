PhD qualifying examination

Timing, registration, course selection, exam format, attempts, and the deadline for passing.

At a glance

WhoAll ECE PhD students.
First attemptNormally during the student's third semester.
Deadline to passNo later than the end of the second year.
When offeredOnce each fall and spring semester, usually in November and March.
RegistrationSubmit the form sent by the Graduate Program Director before the announced deadline.
ExamFour hours, closed book and closed notes. Six questions are provided; the student answers four.
ReexaminationOne reexamination may be permitted following a failure. It requires the examiners' recommendation and the Dean of the Graduate School's approval.

What students need to do

  1. Watch for the registration email

    The Graduate Program Director normally sends the examination date, registration form, and deadline during the first weeks of the semester. A student who misses the registration deadline may not take the examination that semester.

  2. Select three eligible courses

    On the form, identify three ELE courses numbered 500 or above that you completed at URI during your first two semesters in the PhD program. ELE 594 is eligible. ELE 591, 592, 601, 602, 598, 599, and 699 are not eligible.

  3. Take the written examination

    You will receive two questions corresponding to each selected course. Answer four of the six questions. If you answer more than four, clearly identify the four answers that should be evaluated.

  4. Review the result and any next steps

    If you do not pass, a reexamination is not automatic. It must be recommended by the examiners and approved by the Dean of the Graduate School. An approved reexamination normally takes place during the following semester.

Use the semester-specific instructions: the date, form, and registration deadline sent by the Graduate Program Director control for that examination offering.
